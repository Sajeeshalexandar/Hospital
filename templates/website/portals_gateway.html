Enterprise Security & Tenant Isolation Standards
Multi-Tenant Isolation: Data querysets are strictly partitioned per hospital facility with zero cross-tenant leakage.
Clinical Data Privacy: Patient records and electronic health data are strictly restricted to authenticated and authorized personnel.
Backend Role Enforcement: Access permissions and actions are validated server-side on every request and form submission.
Conflict-Free Scheduling: Automated scheduling rules prevent double-booking or doctor availability clashes in real time.
Server-Side Verification: Financial billing and diagnostic lab workflows are computed and verified purely on the server.
Immutable Audit Trail: All administrative updates and security events are committed to a tamper-resistant system log.
