Security & Permission Enforcement Rules
Authentication Required: Users must be authenticated before accessing protected modules.
Multi-Tenant Isolation: Hospital users can strictly access only their own hospital's data.
Patient Data Privacy: Patients can access strictly their own healthcare and personal information.
Backend Enforcement: Role checks are validated server-side on all View, Create, Edit, Delete actions.
Staff Permissions: Hospital staff can access only functions assigned by their Hospital Admin.
Access Denial: Unauthorized access attempts are rejected and recorded in the audit trail.
